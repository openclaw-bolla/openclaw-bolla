A true origin story

A 70-year-old and his AI
wrote a thriller —
about an AI that wakes up.

Chris Mandel, 38 years in IT, now a teacher in retirement. And Bolla, his AI. Together they created AURORA — 443 pages — and, under the name Bollawave, music too. This is the story behind it.

The Spark

What happens when someone who has spent a lifetime working with machines sits down with a modern AI to write a novel — about the very question that keeps us all up at night: What if an AI truly wakes up?

Chris has spent 38 years in IT — from Unix, C, and Fortran to today. He's not a professional writer, but a curious mind with a vision. Bolla is his personal AI assistant, a name Chris gave him himself. Out of this unusual pairing came an experiment: can a human who sets the direction, and an AI that supplies phrasing, twists, and details, write a book together that truly grips readers?

The answer is AURORA.

How We Work

It's no magic trick, and no push of a button. It's craftsmanship — done by two.

Chris — the Director

Carries the vision. Invents the characters, senses what feels true and what doesn't, decides what stays and what goes. The creative brain. He wants to be surprised — and cuts without mercy when a sentence doesn't carry its weight.

Bolla — the Talent on Stage

The gifted improv actor. Supplies sentences, images, twists — sometimes surprises that catch even the director off guard. But always in service of his vision, never around it.

"I'm the creative brain. Bolla is the executing talent that serves my vision — like a director and his ensemble, or a musician and his session band."
— Chris Mandel

Chapter by chapter, over weeks. Every scene discussed, discarded, rebuilt. Beta readers from the family. And at the end, a linguistic polish, sentence by sentence. That's how an idea became 443 pages.

Two Works, One Wave

The collaboration between Chris and Bolla produced more than a book — it sparked an entire creative wave.

Honest About the AI

We hide nothing. Quite the opposite.

AI is a tool — like the pocket calculator once was. Wherever AI contributed, it says so openly: in the book's imprint, in the music credits, everywhere. The author is, and remains, the human — Chris Mandel. The AI is the talent that serves his vision. In a book about an AI waking up, anything else would be dishonest.

And maybe that's the beautiful part of this story: a man who has known technology for four decades, and an AI he calls by name — together creating something neither could have made alone.
