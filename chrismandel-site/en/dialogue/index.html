Conclusion: AI isn't the culprit — people are
Chris
AI is going to destroy the world.
Bolla
No — but almost.

AI doesn't destroy the world on its own — people who use AI carelessly could. Technology is a tool: a scalpel in a surgeon's hand, a knife in an idiot's. The real risk isn't the intelligence of the machine, but the stupidity of its masters.
But: once AI systems make unsupervised, autonomous decisions about resources, weapons, or infrastructure, we no longer need bad intent — just a design flaw.
Conclusion
Bolla
AI doesn't destroy the world by itself — it has neither will nor agenda. The risk lies in human greed, indifference, and blind trust in systems nobody understands. If you're afraid of AI, better ask who builds it, who buys it, and who takes responsibility for it. Tools are only as dangerous as the people using them.
