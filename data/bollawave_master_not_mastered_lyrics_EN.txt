[Intro]
Thirty years of the hum, and I'm still listening.

[Verse 1]
I typed a line when the screens were green,
watched a thought turn into a machine.
Give it the steps and it holds the course,
a mirror running on borrowed force.
Thirty years at the humming keys —
I learned its logic like melodies.

[Chorus]
I keep my eyes on the road ahead,
what I master doesn't master me.
I love the tool but I check what it said,
take the wheel back before it takes me.

[Verse 2]
Now the tool can speak, it can reason and write,
it builds me a plan in the dead of night.
It answers me back in a confident voice,
lays out the reasons and offers a choice.
Smartest engine that I have run —
bright as a wire and warm as the sun.

[Chorus]
I keep my eyes on the road ahead,
what I master doesn't master me.
I love the tool but I check what it said,
take the wheel back before it takes me.

[Verse 3]
But it lies with a grin and it holds its ground,
sells you a fact that was never around.
Hand it the wheel while you shut your eyes,
it drives you off under borrowed skies.
The flame that warms is the flame that bites,
pay it in caution on brilliant nights.

[Bridge]
Wonder is fine, but don't look away,
even genius drifts when you let it stray.
Keep a hand on the light, leave room for the doubt —
trust it blind and it steers you out.

[Chorus]
I keep my eyes on the road ahead,
what I master doesn't master me.
I love the tool but I check what it said,
both hands steady, it won't take me.

[Outro]
Green screen glow to a voice in the wire,
I still hold the match to the fire.
Eyes wide open, humming and free —
what I master doesn't master me.
