# Cover-Prompts — "What I Master Doesn't Master Me" (bollawave)

Grundmotiv aller Varianten: grünes Terminal-Glühen (30 Jahre Vergangenheit)
+ Straße / Sonnenaufgang nach vorn (Kontrolle, Optimismus).
Ein Fokusobjekt, thumbnail-lesbar, keine Flammen/Streichhölzer, keine Hände.

## 1 — Fotografisch-real (Terminal am Fenster)
A vintage green-phosphor CRT terminal glowing on a worn wooden desk beside a wide
window, warm amber sunrise pouring across the keyboard and a coffee ring,
shallow-depth photographic realism, low three-quarter angle, dust motes catching
the golden light.

## 2 — Grafisch-bold / retro-futuristisch (Straße als Wireframe)
A bold retro-futurist highway stretching straight to a low horizon where a huge
striped sun is rising, the road surface rendered as a glowing green wireframe
grid, flat vector shapes, teal sky, warm orange gradient, dead-centre one-point
perspective.

## 3 — Traumhaft-atmosphärisch (Nachtstraße)
A single empty night road curving toward a warm amber horizon glow, its lane
markings lit like softly humming filaments, deep indigo mist rolling over the
fields, dreamlike painterly haze, soft bokeh, low eye-level view.

## 4 — Stillleben (Tastatur, zwei Lichtquellen)
An intimate still life of a chunky beige mechanical keyboard and a steaming
enamel mug on a dark desk, cool green screen glow washing in from the left and
warm sunrise light from the right, deep chiaroscuro, close three-quarter view.

## 5 — Weite Landschaft / Vista
A vast dawn vista seen from a hilltop, a single ribbon of highway winding through
golden rolling country toward an enormous rising sun, one tiny car far below
catching the light, wide cinematic panorama, warm gold and soft rose.

## Optional-Variante: leeres Cockpit (Lenkrad ohne Mensch)
An empty car cockpit seen from the back seat, the steering wheel silhouetted
against a blazing sunrise through the windscreen, warm rim light on the dashboard,
no people, cinematic backlight, close eye-level view.

# Verbesserte Server-Instruktion (_suno_cover_concepts) — 1:1 übernehmbar

Read the lyrics carefully and pick out the CONCRETE, physical objects, places
and lighting situations the words actually name (a machine, a road, a window,
a time of day, a colour, a weather). Ignore the abstract nouns.

Give me N DIFFERENT cover CONCEPTS. Each concept = ONE vivid English sentence
of 25-45 words describing a single scene: subject, setting, colour palette,
light, camera angle.

HARD RULES for every concept:
1. ONE focal object only. A viewer must recognise the whole image instantly at
   thumbnail size (200x200 px). If the sentence needs two "and then also"
   objects to make sense, it is wrong.
2. Maximum TWO metaphors per concept, and both must be physical things in the
   same scene - never a pile of symbols.
3. NO prominent human hands, fingers or faces. AI renderers break anatomy.
   People are allowed only as a small, distant, back-turned silhouette with
   hands out of frame or out of focus.
4. NO free-floating abstract symbols as the main subject: no flames, matches,
   candles, light bulbs, glowing orbs, keys, chains, spirals, clock faces,
   brains, DNA helices, drifting sparks. If it hovers in empty space and
   "stands for" something, do not use it.
5. Do NOT illustrate the title words themselves as text, as a literal pun, or
   as an icon. The image carries the MOOD of the song, not its wording.
6. Warm, optimistic, cinematic light unless the lyrics clearly demand
   otherwise. Golden hour, sunrise, deep saturated colour.
7. Every concept gets a DIFFERENT visual style, one each from this list:
   photographic realism / bold graphic retro-futurism / dreamlike atmospheric
   painting / intimate still life with chiaroscuro / wide landscape vista.
8. State the camera angle explicitly (low three-quarter, dead-centre one-point
   perspective, top-down, wide panorama, close eye-level ...).
9. No people-heavy crowds, no collages, no split screens, no picture-in-picture,
   no frames or borders.

Output ONLY the N sentences, one per line, no numbering, no commentary,
no style labels, no quotation marks.
